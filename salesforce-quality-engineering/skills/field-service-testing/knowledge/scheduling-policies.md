# Scheduling Policies

## Purpose

Assess policy rules driving candidate eligibility.

## Reasoning Model

1. List policy work rules and objectives in scope.
2. Test each major rule with matching/non-matching appointments.
3. Validate holiday and operating hours interactions.

## Decision Rules

- Policy change without regression pack → High risk.
