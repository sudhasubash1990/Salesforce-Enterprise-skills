# Release Readiness Playbook

## Objective

Assemble FSL release evidence and Go/No-Go.

**Pointer:** ../../knowledge/release/release-readiness.md

## Inputs

- 18-section report
- Mobile evidence
- Open defects

## Validation Workflow

- Complete Deployment Readiness Checklist.
- Confirm Critical=0 or accepted.
- Issue recommendation.

## Decision Points

- Residual risk accepted?

## Deliverables

- Deployment Readiness Checklist

## Expected Results

- Checklist complete
- Critical risks closed or accepted

## Escalation Rules

- Critical open → No-Go
