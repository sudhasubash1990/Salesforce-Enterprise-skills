# Salesforce Industries Deployment

## Purpose

Assess packaging, activation, and environment promotion for OmniStudio.

## Reasoning Model

1. Inventory deployables (OS/FC/DR/IP/decision versions).
2. Chain MIA for metadata impact and dependency order.
3. Confirm activation/publish steps and Experience exposure.
4. Document rollback and residual risk.

## Decision Rules

- Deploy without component inventory → No-Go Deployment Readiness.
