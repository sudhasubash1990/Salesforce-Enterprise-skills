# Decision Tables

## Purpose

Validate Decision Table rulesets used in Industries journeys.

## Reasoning Model

1. Inventory rules and evaluation order.
2. Test true/false and multi-match behavior.
3. Confirm OS/IP consumption of results.
4. Label org-specific rule names as TBC when unknown.

## Decision Rules
