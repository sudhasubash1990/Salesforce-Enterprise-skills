# Conversation Design

## Purpose

Validate conversation UX: clarity, tone, next steps.

## Reasoning Model

1. Check greeting, clarification, confirmation, close.
2. Align tone to brand and persona.
3. Verify actionable next steps.

## Decision Rules

- Vague answers without next step → Fail UX criterion.
