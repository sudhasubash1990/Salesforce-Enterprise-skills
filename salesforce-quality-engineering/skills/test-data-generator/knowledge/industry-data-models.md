# Industry Data Models

## Purpose

Shape synthetic data with industry context without inventing compliance.

## Reasoning Model

1. Select industry scenario pack.
2. Map industry objects only when licensed/confirmed.
3. Cross-link industry knowledge.
4. Flag regulatory themes as TBC.

## Decision Rules

- Do not invent HIPAA/PCI certifications.
